# Business Questions

## Executive Analytics

- What is the total transaction volume?
- What is the total transaction value?
- How many active customers are transacting?
- What is the fraud rate by day?
- What transaction types drive the most value?
- How much transaction value is associated with fraud?

## Operations Analytics

- Which transaction types occur most frequently?
- Are there balance inconsistencies by transaction type?
- How does activity change across simulated days and hours?
- Which customers or recipients show unusually high activity?

## Fraud And Risk Analytics

- What behaviors distinguish fraudulent transactions from legitimate ones?
- Are fraudulent transactions concentrated in specific transaction types?
- Are fraudulent transactions unusually large?
- Do fraudulent transactions deplete sender balances?
- Do risky customers transact with many unique recipients?
- Do high-risk transactions show balance reconciliation issues?

## Primary Investigation Question

What behavioral characteristics distinguish fraudulent transactions from legitimate transactions?
